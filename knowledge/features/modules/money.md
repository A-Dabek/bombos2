# Money Flows Feature Module

The Money Flows feature manages recurring income and expense flows to track cash flow patterns.

## Core Capabilities & Workflows

1. **Flow Configuration**: Define recurring income/expense amounts, titles, and recurrence rules.
2. **Administration**: Add, edit, or delete money flow entries.
