# Groceries Feature Module

The Groceries feature manages grocery list planning, shopping list execution, product category filtering, and frequent purchase counting.

## Core Capabilities & Workflows

1. **Planning Mode**: Add and organize planned items with quantities and product categories.
2. **Shopping Mode**: Interactive shopping checklist grouped by product categories with quick completion toggles.
3. **Auto-Suggestions**: Product suggestions based on historical purchase counts.
